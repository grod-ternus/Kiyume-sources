## How to add it

1. On your iPhone or iPad, open **[this page](https://grod-ternus.github.io/Kiyume-sources/install.html)** and tap **Add to Kiyume**.
2. Kiyume opens and asks if you want to add the list. Tap **Add**.
3. Choose the sources you like in **Explore** and start reading.

Don't have Kiyume yet? **[Get it on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/dWuRaJqU)**, then come back.

## What's inside

Sources in English, Italian, Spanish, French, Indonesian, Turkish, Portuguese and more, covering manga, manhwa, manhua, webtoons and Western comics. Each one shows a short description, what it publishes and a rating out of 7 before you install it.

## Is it safe?

Yes. A source only tells Kiyume where to find series on a website; no content is stored here. Nothing is installed until you confirm, and the whole list is signed, so it can't be changed by anyone else.
